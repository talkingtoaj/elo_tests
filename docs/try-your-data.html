Try your data

Have a spreadsheet of past answers? Drop it in. The library will tell you how clean your answers are, which questions look like they sit in the wrong band, and which band each student is in. It all runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

1. Your answers

A CSV file with four columns, one row per answer: student, question, band, correct. For correct use 1/0, yes/no or right/wrong. Save as CSV from Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

What you get

  1. A plain summary of how clean your answers are, and what that means for test length.
  2. Flagged questions. Questions whose answers fit a different band than the one you listed. Needs about 150 students.
  3. A band for each student, with a range and how sure it is.
  4. Three CSV files to download and use in your own course software.

No data? Press "Use the sample file". It has 200 made-up students on a 10-band course, with three questions planted in the wrong band.
